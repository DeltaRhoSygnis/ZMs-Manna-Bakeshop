# Zm's Manna Bakeshop — Copy Deck

Working copy for the site. Everything here uses only the facts the owner supplied:
location, hours, phone, DTI registration, 100 g heat-sealed pouches, the six product
lines, and the tagline "Love at first bite." Anything not supplied is held back and
listed in section 10.

---

## 1. Voice in five lines

1. Sound like the neighbor who bakes: plain words, short sentences, no selling voice.
2. Lead with texture and taste. "Glassy sugar crust" does more than "irresistible."
3. Use local words where they are true: merienda, pasalubong, kape, tsokolate, sari-sari.
4. Never claim what the shop cannot show: no years, no awards, no health talk.
5. Never use: "artisanal", "handcrafted with passion", "elevate your merienda".

## 6. Product cards

Card line goes under the photo, max 12 words. Detail copy fills the popup, 30 to 45
words, and follows the same order: what it is, how it feels, what to drink with it.

### Cookie de Longa
- **Card line (8 words):** Sesame cookie sandwiches with a soft caramel center
- **Detail (37 words):** Two round cookies, crumbly at the edge and soft at the middle, pressed around a sweet signature filling and topped with toasted sesame. Butter and caramel sugar come through first. Best with barako coffee or warm milk.

### Biscocho
- **Card line (7 words):** Twice-baked bread with a glassy sugar crust
- **Detail (35 words):** Sliced bread baked once, glazed with butter and sugar, then baked again until deep gold. The crust cracks like glass and the middle stays light. It is our top seller. Morning coffee or hot tsokolate.

### Toasted Mammon
- **Card line (6 words):** Slow-toasted sponge with an airy crunch
- **Detail (35 words):** Light vanilla sponge, sliced thin and toasted slowly until the edges crisp and the inside stays airy. There is a little caramel in the finish. Lighter than biscocho, and it goes fast with afternoon kape.

### Patatas
- **Card line (6 words):** Savory-sweet biscuit batons for black coffee
- **Detail (33 words):** Finger-length biscuit batons with a firm snap and a fine crumb. Savory at the edges, lightly sweet underneath, with a hint of salt at the end. The merienda classic, best with black coffee.

### Hopia Choco
- **Card line (7 words):** Flaky pockets packed with chocolate bean paste
- **Detail (33 words):** A layered pastry shell that flakes apart in your hand, filled with a dense, fudgy chocolate bean paste. The crust stays crisp, the center stays soft and rich. Warm milk or afternoon kape.

### Otap & Loaf Breads
- **Card line (7 words):** Crisp otap puffs and soft sliced loaves
- **Detail (42 words):** Two things in one line: oval otap puffs that shatter at the first bite, and soft sliced loaf bread for breakfast. The otap keeps in its pouch; the loaf is best eaten fresh, with butter, jam, or a spread of your choice.

## 9. Microcopy

- **Menu button:** `Menu` (visible), `Open menu` / `Close menu` (aria-label, swaps with state)
- **Back to top:** `Back to top` (aria-label and tooltip); visible glyph stays `↑`
- **Empty state, no products found:** `No products to show here yet. Call 0926-177-4601 to ask what is in stock.`
- **Popup close:** `Close details`
- **Photo viewer:** `Previous photo` / `Next photo` / `Close photo viewer`
- **Pouch line, reusable under product photos:** `100 g heat-sealed pouch, labeled and ready to resell.`

### Image alt-text patterns

Three patterns, 8 to 15 words each. Name the product, describe only what is visible,
never open with "image of" or "photo of".

1. **Card photo:** `Zm's Manna {product} in a clear pouch, {one visible detail}.`
   Example: `Zm's Manna biscocho slices stacked in a clear pouch, sugar crust catching the light.`
2. **Popup photo:** `{product} close up, showing {texture detail}.`
   Example: `Cookie de Longa close up, toasted sesame on a soft caramel center.`
3. **Label or shelf photo:** `The printed Zm's Manna label on a {product} pouch, showing the DTI registration and barcode.`

Add "100 g" only when the pouch front is legible in the shot. The Otap and loaf card
uses the logo, so its alt text describes the card, not a product photo.
