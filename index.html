Distribution & Logistics Details

Operating guidelines for retail store deliveries, freight pickups, and provincial dispatch across Mindanao.

pin_drop Primary Service Area & Coverage
expand_more
Based in Valencia City, Bukidnon. Regular scheduled dispatches serve Bukidnon municipalities (Malaybalay, Maramag, Quezon, Manolo Fortich) and onward logistics freight corridors connecting Cagayan de Oro City, Davao Region, and Northern Mindanao.
local_shipping Delivery Methods & Pickup Options
expand_more
Three flexible channels: 1) Direct local route delivery for recurring merchant partners in Valencia; 2) Third-party regional logistics / cargo couriers for provincial store shipments; and 3) Direct warehouse pickup at P-2 Lumbo for local travelers and wholesalers.
inventory Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ)
expand_more
Tier-based per box carton bundle: Sample bundles start at 20 pouches; standard reseller starter cases at 50 assorted 100g packs; commercial master cartons at 100 to 300+ units with tiered wholesale margins.
schedule Production & Batch Baking Lead Time
expand_more
Standard wholesale batch orders require 2 to 4 business days advance notice to allow fresh dough batching, oven baking, cooling, heat-sealing, barcode quality-checking, and protective master carton packing.
payments Payment Terms & Reseller Margins
expand_more
We support direct bank transfer, GCash business accounts, and Cash on Delivery (COD) for established local retail partners within Bukidnon. Wholesale price sheets include generous suggested retail price (SRP) spreads.

Baking Quality Biscuits for Mindanao Homes & Stores

Located in P-2 Lumbo, Valencia City, Bukidnon, Zm’s Manna Bakeshop has built a dependable reputation as a local producer of traditional toasted breads, crispy cookies, and signature pasalubong delicacies.

We combine time-tested recipes with modern clear pouch packaging to guarantee crispy crunch and genuine taste. From daily family merienda to tourist travel gift bags, our products proudly embody Valencia’s rich baking heritage.
