Our story

A neighborhood bakehouse, baked with heart.

Zm’s Manna Bakeshop is a local bakehouse in P-2 Lumbo, Valencia City, Bukidnon. We bake the snacks Mindanao grew up with — twice-baked biscocho, toasted mammon, hopia, and buttery cookies — in small batches, then seal them fresh the same day.

Every pouch carries our DTI-registered label and a ready-to-scan barcode, so the same favorites you enjoy at home are right at rest on the shelves of local stores and pasalubong counters across Bukidnon.

How we bake

From dough to sealed pouch

Four steps, no shortcuts — the same routine every baking day.

01

Mix & shape

Time-tested recipes mixed and shaped by hand in small batches — never mass-produced.

02

Bake low & slow

Even, golden bakes that build flavor and set the crumb before toasting.

03

Toast for crunch

Biscocho and mammon go back in the oven — twice-baked for that signature crisp.

04

Cool & seal

Fully cooled, then heat-sealed in labeled 100g pouches the same day.

Freshness & packaging

Made to stay crisp

How every pouch protects the bake — from our oven to your pantry.

Heat-sealed pouches

Clear, moisture-proof packs keep biscuits crisp through transport and shelf time.

Honest labels

DTI registration, nutrition details, and a scannable barcode printed on every unit.

Uniform 100g packs

The same convenient size across the biscuit lines — easy to grab, easy to share.

Shelf-stable crunch

Store cool and dry, reseal after opening, and the crunch holds for weeks.

Order from us

  • Pasalubong and reseller supply — by the carton or by the dozen.
