Supply & order fulfillment

Distribution & Logistics

Guidelines for store deliveries, freight pickups, and provincial dispatch.

Primary service area & coverage

Based in Valencia City, Bukidnon. Regular dispatches serve Malaybalay, Maramag, Quezon, and Manolo Fortich, with freight corridors to Cagayan de Oro, Davao Region, and Northern Mindanao.

Delivery methods & pickup options

Local route delivery for recurring partners, third-party cargo couriers for provincial stores, and direct warehouse pickup at P-2 Lumbo.

Minimum order quantities

Sample bundles start at 20 pouches, reseller starter cases at 50 packs, and commercial cartons at 100 to 300+ units.

Production & lead time

Wholesale batches need 2 to 4 business days for baking, cooling, sealing, barcode checking, and carton packing.

Payment terms

Bank transfer, GCash business accounts, and COD for established Bukidnon partners. Price sheets include suggested retail margins.
