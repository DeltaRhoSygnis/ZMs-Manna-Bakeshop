Packaging & Merchandising Specifications

Engineered for long ambient shelf-life, simple inventory scanning, and attractive display placement.

  • Clear Heat-Sealed Pouches: Durable plastic with ingredients, allergen labels, and clear expiry date stamp.
  • Barcode & DTI Ready: Designated slot for official DTI Negosyo Center branding and standard POS barcodes.
  • Standard Net Weight: Approximately ~100g per retail pouch.
  • Long Shelf Life: Great for hanging display strips, convenience store counters, and grocery shelves.

About Zm's Manna Bakeshop

Our bakery story, facility, and location in Valencia City.

Bakery Overview

Located in P-2 Lumbo, Valencia City, Bukidnon, Zm's Manna Bakeshop is a dedicated local producer of traditional toasted breads, cookies, otap, and pasalubong bakery items.

We serve walk-in customers, mini marts, supermarkets, and local resellers across Bukidnon with freshly baked products.
